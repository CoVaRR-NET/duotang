

Data on cases and genomic sampling has been limited, however the data available to date are consistent with current case levels trending lower, involving primarily a combination of <u id='XFG'>XFG</u> subvariants and PQ subvariants (descended from <u id='NB_1_8_1'>NB.1.8.1</u>), plus some <u id='BA_3_2'>BA.3.2</u> subvariants at lower levels in clinically relevant cases. No clear, single variant is driving any new increase in clinically significant cases, in the provinces reporting to date.



Variants of current interest (due to their current/potential growth advantage, mutations of potential functional significance, or spread in other countries):

* <u id='PQ_2_1'>PQ.2.1</u> subvariants (descended from <u id='NB_1_8_1'>NB.1.8.1</u>)
* <u id='PQ_2_8_1'>PQ.2.8.1</u>, or subvariants (includes RC.** variants)
* Subvariants of <u id='XFG'>XFG</u>, including <u id='XFG_1_1'>XFG.1.1</u>/<u id='XFG_1_1_1'>XFG.1.1.1</u>
* <u id='PE_1_4'>PE.1.4</u>
* <u id='BA_3_2'>BA.3.2</u>, of interest due in part to evidence it is disproportionately affecting children. Also, it has been less detected in clinical cases verses wastewater). 

We continue to monitor for, but have not recently detected, any other highly divergent variants in Canada (“saltation” lineages with a sudden increase in number of mutations).



Note reductions or delays in data can cause delays in the detection of new variants and more uncertainty when estimating selection.
